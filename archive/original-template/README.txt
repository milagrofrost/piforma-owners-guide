PiForma Owner's Guide V2

FILES
- template.typ: editable Typst source; keep bundled artwork PNG in same folder.
- piforma-artwork.png: reliable raster asset for Typst. Replace with your real PiForma image once ready.
- piforma-artwork.svg: editable illustration master.
- PiForma-Owners-Guide-print.pdf: four page proof rendered with ReportLab at 6.10 x 7.30 inches.
- preview.png: four pages at a glance.
- build_pdf.py: reproducible PDF layout renderer (requires reportlab and Pillow).

IMPORTANT
The PDF was rendered from the included Python layout renderer, not directly compiled from Typst. Typst is hand-authored to match the design, but has not been compiled or validated here. It may need minor adjustment. The typography and exact line wraps may differ.

Print at 100% actual size, double-sided if desired, 6.10 x 7.30 inch trimmed, with sufficient inside margin for coil binding. Ask the shop whether they can trim and punch the desired edge. No fake spiral perforations printed.
