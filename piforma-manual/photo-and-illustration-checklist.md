## What style to send

**You can send ordinary unlabelled originals. You do not need to produce finished outlines first.**

- **Hardware photographs:** full-resolution color originals, plain background, even lighting, sharp focus and room around the object. Photograph the whole connection as well as a separate connector close-up. Keep complete objects/cord ends inside the frame.
- **Interior hardware artwork:** generally B&W outlines with restrained red leaders and black labels. Use true grayscale photographs when texture, a display, small markings or a mechanism would be clearer than an outline.
- **Software:** native PNG screenshots from the actual PiForma, not camera photographs of the screen. Retain the native resolution; do not enlarge/upscale first. Keep a clean original and optionally a marked-up copy showing what you want labeled. Grayscale is the default final treatment; retain color when it genuinely communicates status.
- **Indicators and model labels:** actual photos rather than outlines. Get close enough for the symbols/lettering to be readable. Provide context plus a close-up so the location is not ambiguous.
- **Finished drawings, if you prefer making them:** editable SVG with separate labels is ideal. PNG remains usable; aim for enough resolution for its intended size. Preserve the original and keep lettering readable at the eventual small page size.
- **Naming:** the shot-list IDs are convenient filenames, for example `S-03-save-dialog.png` or `H-04-handle-reaching.jpg`. Add a short description of what each view shows and any verified behavior it demonstrates.

Keep the pictures focused on real procedures and recognizable parts. We will choose the smallest useful set and reuse views across chapters rather than grow the book for the sake of photographs. The full editorial uncertainties remain in `editorial-production-tracker.md`.
