# Welcome to the Macintosh PiForma

<!-- evidence: owner-current H04; repo-verified D-README D-HARDWARE -->
Welcome to the Macintosh PiForma.

This little computer began as an Apple IIc Monitor, a Raspberry Pi, and a willingness to spend rather more time on the combination than common sense might recommend. The old monitor’s case now holds a modern display and a battery-powered computer, with a familiar-looking desktop, games, and a few diversions that have earned their place mostly by being amusing.

PiForma is a one-off experiment, not a Macintosh manufactured by Apple. Its name is a small joke; its carrying bag is a surprisingly serious undertaking. You can use it without knowing how the wiring works, although you will want to know which way the keyboard goes back into the bag.

This guide starts with the things you need to touch, connect, and switch on. Later chapters explain the parts and programs behind the appearance. There is no required course in Raspberry Pi administration before your first game.

# About this manual

<!-- evidence: editorial-advice OUTLINE -->
Chapter 1, “Getting Started,” explains how to unpack the computer, arrange the equipment, check the battery, start up, and stop. It also puts everything back into the bag in the right order. Taking things out is easier than remembering where they belonged.

Chapter 2, “Inside the Macintosh PiForma,” introduces the monitor shell, Raspberry Pi, display, battery, controls, keyboard, mouse, speakers, and bag. It explains what the parts do before getting into the finer details.

Chapter 3, “How the Macintosh PiForma Works,” introduces the desktop, launcher, menu bar, games, and other programs. It distinguishes the computer’s physical controls from the menus on its screen.

Chapter 4, “Caring for Your Macintosh PiForma,” covers handling, cleaning, charging, storage, and transport. It includes the brief but comprehensive description of the service department.

Appendix A collects technical records and points to the project documentation. Appendix B helps you find a useful check when something goes wrong. The glossary explains unfamiliar terms used in the guide.
