# Questions for the owner, prioritized

Do not block drafting on all of these. Keep `[PHOTO NEEDED]`, `[VERIFY]`, or `[MODEL TO CONFIRM]` placeholders in sections until answers arrive.

## Highest priority for accuracy

1. **Bose speakers and stands:** What exact model are the two Bose speakers? Are they powered, what cables connect them to PiForma, and where are the stands stored? Are they part of the same bag system or separate?
2. **Travel bag:** Can we photograph the actual packing sequence including the special foam indents? Is the smaller brown pouch felt or velvet, or both? Where does charging power/charging pad sit relative to the bag?
3. **Power:** What is the actual battery model, charger rating and wireless charging arrangement? How does the battery plunger behave: show level, wake pack, or both?
4. **Apple-logo control:** What does the *current* code do for one/two/three/four taps and 2, 6 and 10-second holds? Test full-screen RetroPie exit. Older notes disagree.
5. **Startup chime:** Does rotating the dial while powered on play sample chimes or only choose the next startup chime? Which positions are blank and intentional?
6. **Audio-in:** What is meant by 'audio output to USB' feeding PiForma audio input? Identify adapter model and signal direction.
7. **N64 controllers:** Are these native N64 controllers plus USB adapters or USB versions? Which apps use them?
8. **Keyboard:** Verify switch position, Bluetooth pairing procedure, battery indicators, and whether Ctrl+Esc is the saved DOSBox-X mouse-release binding.
9. **Shutdown:** What exact item in the real desktop menu performs shutdown? Does the right-side power switch instantly remove power?
10. **Hardware internals:** Can source repositories or photographs confirm donor monitor model, GPIO wiring and parts list?
