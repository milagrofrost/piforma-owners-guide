# Photograph and diagram checklist

Do not fabricate visual appearance of undocumented parts. The owner intends to photograph PiForma and have photos transformed into period-style technical line art.

## Essential for Chapter 1

- [ ] Carry bag open, upright, all contents in place
- [ ] Top-down layer-by-layer sequence, one shot per layer
- [ ] Small brown accessory pouch open showing two N64 controllers, USB hub and power cable
- [ ] PiForma inside bag, screen up, keyboard tucked at side
- [ ] Close-up of keyboard position showing keys toward bag wall and thicker edge toward rear
- [ ] Hand reaching down inner front wall to pull out PiForma handle
- [ ] Bag on its back showing alternate removal angle
- [ ] Foam insert's molded indents, machine aligned properly and intentionally misaligned for comparison
- [ ] Charging arrangement/indicators to document when alignment is correct
- [ ] Folding stand open, empty and with PiForma centered on it
- [ ] Complete working setup: PiForma, keyboard, mouse and pad, speakers and stands
- [ ] Right-side power switch, front green light, chime selector on back
- [ ] Top opened safely showing battery button/plunger and battery-level LEDs
- [ ] Rear connectors, especially the single accessible USB port and charger input
- [ ] Keyboard on/off switch; mouse underside on/off switch; hub with controllers connected
- [ ] Actual desktop startup and About This PiForma icon, after boot

## Line-art guidance

Use black ink line art, sparse hatching or modest halftone, clean leader lines, labels, figure numbers and natural product proportions. Prefer tracing exact owner photographs or existing SVG/CAD models. Do not introduce fictional ports, dimensions, cables or labels. Keep high-resolution photo originals alongside final cleaned drawings.
