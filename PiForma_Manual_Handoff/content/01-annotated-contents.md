# Proposed table of contents

**Working title: Macintosh PiForma Owner's Guide**

The organization follows the Macintosh Plus Owner's Guide, but removes obsolete or irrelevant sections and inserts PiForma-specific material. Section names are working labels, not mandatory literal reproductions.

## Front matter

- **Welcome to the Macintosh PiForma**: brief personal introduction, what this project is, why it exists, and what to expect.
- **About this manual**: one paragraph per chapter, similar to Apple's original approach. No 'About the other manuals' or 'Using the manuals'.
- **Contents**: generated from headings. Preface may use roman numerals if it suits layout.
- **Credits and acknowledgments**: respectfully credit projects, hardware designs, community code and source material; do not imply the author invented the hardware/software or that Apple built PiForma. Include links in a low-key end section.
