## Starting up

1. Locate the **physical power button on the right side** of PiForma and press/engage it.
2. A **green light at the front** should illuminate.
3. Depending on chime-selector position, a chime may sound.
4. A startup picture/sequence should appear; the desktop normally follows within approximately **one to two minutes**.

The green indicator/chime/screen sequence is important for troubleshooting. The chime-selector dial is on the **back** and includes some silent/empty settings. While powered on, move the dial through several positions in either direction, listening for an available sound. The owner's account says this may trigger or select a chime while powered on; precise behavior must be tested before final wording.

## Checking the battery and first-start recovery

The **top of the PiForma opens** to expose a battery-check mechanism. The outer **plunger** mechanically presses a button on the internal battery pack, showing its charge indicators. It can also awaken/enable the battery in some circumstances, according to the owner. If the machine doesn't turn on:

- Check battery status using the plunger as designed.
- Press the plunger to enable/wake the battery if necessary.
- Plug in external power if the pack is empty, apparently asleep or refuses to start despite showing charge. The owner reports that it sometimes starts after no more than roughly one minute on external power, even when previously reading full.
- Try the power switch again and look for front light, chime and display.

Do **not** promise that one minute always works, or say a failed chime definitively means hardware failure. Treat this as an observed oddity, not a known electrical explanation.
